      How it works

      1. A robot visits every site

        About every 5 minutes, it loads each homepage, and the sign-in page for banks, from GitHub's cloud servers. No answer, or a server error, after a retry: Down. Some pages failing, or very slow: Having issues.

      2. It reads the news

        Some outages never show on a homepage, like a bank's login breaking or an agency's internal network failing. When several outlets report a problem in the last few hours, and nothing newer says it's fixed: Reported in the news.

      3. It checks official status pages

        When a company's own status page reports a major outage, that counts too. Minor incidents are shown as notes.

      4. You can test it yourself

        Is it just me? tries every site from your own browser. If most fail for you but not for us, the problem is probably your connection.

      Sites that block robots but still answer count as working. Phone and home-internet networks can't be checked from a website, so carriers are tracked from news reports only. Something wrong or missing? Tell us on GitHub.
